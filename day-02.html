SELECT and WHERE

WHERE, comparison operators, AND, OR and the parentheses trap, on the 20-row employees table from the Day 2 setup. Predict first, then run it and watch rows drop out. Watch the Day 2 video.

The tables are the ones from the Day 2 setup script, running in SQLite inside your browser. The course itself uses PostgreSQL, so a few things display differently: SQLite shows TRUE/FALSE as 1/0 (shown here as t/f, the way psql prints them), drops trailing zeros (shown here with two decimals where the column is NUMERIC), and words its error messages differently. Every result on this page was checked against the documented output when the page was built. Course repo.
